Coach's notes

Right now he rides about 180 km a week at roughly 24 km/h. The goal needs 30.8 km/h for over three hours. That's a big jump, so the plan builds it in three ways: more aerobic engine (long easy rides up to 120 km), a higher ceiling (threshold and hard intervals), and lots of time at 31 km/h so goal pace feels normal by February.

The real fitness is built before 6 Feb. Ramzan is for holding onto it, not adding to it. The 12 days off before the race cost very little: two weeks of rest loses only a small slice of endurance, and he'll arrive fresh.

His week: Mon ride · Tue swim · Wed ride · Thu swim · Fri rest · Sat long ride · Sun strength or rest. Every interval ride starts with 15 min easy and ends with 10 min easy. Strength is 20 min at home on Sundays until 5 Feb, plus a second round after the Tuesday swim if he has the energy: 3 rounds of squats, lunges, glute bridges, plank and side plank.

Checkpoint: the 100 km rehearsal on Sat 30 Jan. Under 3:20 there means 3:15 is on for race day, with a fresh body and a group to ride with.

Swimming · 35 m pool

Two swims a week build his engine and breathing control with zero pounding on the legs. The Tuesday swim loosens the legs after Monday's ride, and Friday stays a full rest day. He's a beginner, so for the first two months the swims are about technique and comfort, not distance.

Tuesday is skills: kicking, breathing and stroke drills. Thursday builds continuous swimming, from single lengths in November to 20 lengths (700 m) non-stop by 4 Feb.

  • 1 length = 35 m. The plan counts in lengths, so he never has to do maths at the wall.
  • Rest at the wall as long as he needs. Slow and smooth beats fast and splashy.
  • Breathe out underwater through the nose and mouth, so the breath in at the side is quick and easy.
  • Swim only when a lifeguard is on duty. If he can't yet swim one length unaided, book 4–6 lessons and use them as the swim days.
  • Cycling comes first. If his legs are wrecked, skip the swim, not the ride.

Race-day plan

  • First 10 km: 29–30 km/h. It will feel too easy. That's correct.
  • Km 10–80: settle at 31–32 km/h, RPE 6–7. Sit in a group whenever there is one: drafting saves a quarter of the effort.
  • Climbs: stay at RPE 7 or below and accept losing speed. Pedal the descents.
  • Km 80–100: if the checkpoint times are on, empty the tank from km 90.
  • Eat: 60–80 g carbs an hour, starting at km 10. A gel, half a banana or 3 dates every 25 min.
  • Drink: 500–750 ml an hour with electrolytes. Two bottles plus a refill at halfway.

Last 12 days

  • 8–14 Mar: no riding. Eid: eat well and get carbs back in. 30 min walk and 15 min stretching daily.
  • 15–17 Mar: if he can get on a bike once, 30 min easy with 3 × 1 min at goal pace. If not, that's fine.
  • 18–19 Mar: carb load: rice, roti, potatoes, bananas, about 8 g of carbs per kg of body weight a day. Less fibre on the 19th.
  • 19 Mar: check tyres, chain and brakes. Pack kit, bottles and food. Early night.
  • 20 Mar: breakfast 3 hours before the start. Nothing new on race day.
